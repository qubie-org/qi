Researches a question and lays the answer out as slides.

The same process `/research` runs — several searches, more than one source read,
a gate on whether a page answers the question at all, and a quote checked
character by character against the page it came from. What changes is the shape
at the end: a note is something to come back to, a deck is something to show.

Every slide is one finding. Under it sits the sentence it came from, verbatim,
and the page that sentence was on. Nothing on a slide was written freehand — the
model chooses a title and a heading for each slide, both a few words long and
both under a grammar, and everything else is copied through from claims that
already survived checking.

The last slide says what was set aside and why. A deck that shows only what it
found reads as complete whether or not it is, and the difference between "this
is what there is" and "this is what I could get" is worth one slide.

Opens when it is done. `@deck` has the rest of them.
