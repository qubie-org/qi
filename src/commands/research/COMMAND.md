Investigates a question properly and leaves a note behind.

Not a search. A search asks once and believes the first answer; this asks the
question several ways, reads what comes back, keeps only the sources that
actually address it, and makes every claim point at the sentence it came from.

Takes tens of seconds. That is the cost of reading eight pages instead of one,
and it reports what it is doing as it goes.

The note lands in `@note` — every claim carries its quote and its link, so
anything it got wrong is visible rather than merely wrong.
